This Privacy Policy explains what information Walletalk collects, how it is used and the choices available to you. It applies to the Walletalk apps, web app, Walletalk mini apps and this website, operated by Walletalk Inc. (“Walletalk”, “we”).

1. What Walletalk never collects

Walletalk never receives your seed phrase, your private keys, your wallet password or your backup password. For a Keyless Wallet, Walletalk holds one key share that cannot sign on its own; the recovery file is encrypted on your device with your backup password before it is relayed to your email, and Walletalk cannot decrypt it.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address or phone number, nickname, avatar and settings.
  • Messages and media: messages, files and call signaling needed to deliver them and keep them in sync across your devices. Burn-after-reading messages are deleted after they are read.
  • Wallet information: public addresses, the receiving address linked to your account, and records of transfers, requests, red packets and swaps made through Walletalk. Public blockchain data is visible to anyone.
  • Device and usage information: device model, operating system, app version, IP address, crash logs and push notification tokens.
  • Support conversations: messages you exchange with Customer Service.

3. How we use it

  • to provide, sync and secure the Service, including security emails and the security hold;
  • to detect and prevent fraud, scams and abuse;
  • to send notifications you have enabled;
  • to provide support and improve the Service;
  • to meet legal obligations.

Walletalk does not sell personal information.

4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help run the Service (such as cloud hosting, email delivery, push notifications and blockchain node providers) under contracts that limit their use of it; with mini apps you choose to connect, limited to what you approve; and when required by law or to protect users from harm.

5. Notifications

You control whether notifications show message content. Push notifications are delivered through the notification services of your device platform.

6. Retention

We keep information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your information unless the law requires us to keep it. Data written to public blockchains cannot be deleted.

7. Your choices

You can update your profile, adjust notification and privacy settings, request a copy of your information, or delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

8. International transfers

Walletalk serves users in many countries, so information may be processed outside your country. We protect it with safeguards appropriate to the transfer.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We will announce material changes to this policy in the app or on this website before they take effect.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: Customer Service in the app
