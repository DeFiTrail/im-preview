Getting started4

What is Walletalk?
A messenger with a built-in crypto wallet. Chats, groups and group live sit next to transfers, payment requests and red packets, along with mini apps such as Walletalk Swap and Coin Wiki.
How do I sign up?
Download the app or open the web app, then sign up with an email address or phone number. The same account works on every device.
Is Walletalk free?
Yes. Chats and the wallet are free to use. On-chain transactions carry network fees, and Swap shows its service fee before confirming. A paid membership with extra perks is on the way.
Which devices are supported?
Android 6.0 or later, iOS 15 or later through TestFlight and later the App Store, and the web app in current versions of Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox.

Wallets8

Identity Wallet or Keyless Wallet?
An Identity Wallet suits anyone comfortable keeping a 12-word seed phrase. A Keyless Wallet skips the seed phrase: a backup password protects a recovery file sent to the linked email. Both types can live in the same account.
How do I back up an Identity Wallet?
Write down the 12-word seed phrase in order and keep it offline. Whoever holds these words controls the wallet, and Walletalk cannot restore them.
The Keyless Wallet backup password is lost. What now?
The backup password cannot be recovered. While a device with the wallet still works, set a new one from the wallet’s Security tab. The old recovery file then stops working.
I forgot the wallet password.
Choose “Forgot wallet password” on the unlock screen. This clears all wallet data on this device and sets a new password. Identity Wallets are imported again with their seed phrase; Keyless Wallets sync from another device or restore with the recovery file. Any wallet without a backup is listed before anything is cleared.
My phone is lost.
Identity Wallet: import the seed phrase on a new device. Keyless Wallet: restore with the recovery file and backup password. Restoring starts a 48-hour security hold and notifies the old device, which can cancel the restore if it wasn’t the owner.
Can I export the private key?
Yes. An Identity Wallet exports its seed phrase or private key after the wallet password. A Keyless Wallet exports the full private key with the wallet password, recovery file, backup password and an email code, even if Walletalk stops running.
Which networks are supported?
EVM networks such as Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base and Arbitrum, plus Solana and Tron. More networks are on the way.
Can one account hold several wallets?
Yes, of both types. The default wallet is used for in-chat transfers, red packets and mini apps. Change it in the wallet’s Security tab, or switch for a single transfer.

Payments5

How do I send money in a chat?
Open the chat, choose Transfer, pick the token, network and amount, then confirm with the wallet password or biometrics. The transfer shows up as a message and updates as it settles.
How do payment requests work?
Send a request with an amount and a note. The other side pays with one tap, and the request is marked paid automatically.
What kinds of red packets are there?
Lucky-draw packets split a total at random. Packets can also be locked with a passphrase, or reserved for wallets that hold a chosen token.
What fees apply?
Network fees go to the blockchain and are shown before confirming. Walletalk Swap adds a service fee, also shown before confirming.
Where do I see where a payment came from?
Wallet history labels each payment with the chat it happened in. Tap the label to jump back to the conversation.

Groups and live3

Who can start a group live?
Group owners and admins with an eligible membership can start and schedule a live. Everyone in the group can watch and raise a hand to join the mic.
How does translation work?
Turn on Auto Translate in a chat, or translate a single message from its menu.
How does Burn after Reading work?
Turn it on for a chat and pick a timer. Messages disappear after they’ve been read.

Security4

Has Walletalk been audited?
The wallet is audited by CertiK before public launch. The full report is published on the Security page.
I received a “Not me” email.
If the action wasn’t started by the account owner, tap “Not me”. Signing is frozen until the owner finishes from a trusted device.
What is the security hold?
Restoring a Keyless Wallet on a new device starts a 48-hour hold. Funds can arrive, nothing can leave, and the old device is notified.
Someone asked for my seed phrase.
It’s a scam. Walletalk never asks for seed phrases, private keys, passwords or recovery files. Stop replying and report the account to Customer Service.
