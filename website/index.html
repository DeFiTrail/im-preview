Can’t sign alone

Any two shares sign. Device-to-device sync is end-to-end encrypted. Even if Walletalk stops running, the recovery file and backup password still export the full private key.

Password and biometrics

Everyday signing asks for the wallet password (12+ characters) or biometrics.

“Not me” alerts

Sensitive wallet actions send an email with a one-tap link that freezes signing.

48-hour security hold

Restoring on a new device starts a hold: funds can arrive, nothing can leave, and the old device is notified.

Scam signals in chat

Risk checks on brand-new accounts and bursts of friend requests help stop scams early.

Help Center
Is Walletalk free?

Yes. Chats and the wallet are free to use. On-chain transactions carry network fees, and Swap shows its fee before confirming. A paid membership with extra perks is on the way.

Who holds the keys?

An Identity Wallet’s seed phrase and private keys are created on the device and never leave it. A Keyless Wallet’s key is split into three shares: one on the device, one in an encrypted recovery file, one with the platform. The platform’s share can never sign alone, so the platform cannot move assets on its own.

Which networks are supported?

EVM networks such as Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base and Arbitrum, plus Solana and Tron. More networks are on the way.

How can I tell the app is official?

Download only from this website or official app stores. Each Android release lists a SHA-256 checksum here, so the file can be checked before installing.

Is there an iPhone app?

Yes. Install the iOS app through TestFlight; the App Store listing follows. The web app also runs in Safari on iPhone.
