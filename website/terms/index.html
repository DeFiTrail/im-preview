3. Self-custodial wallets

Walletalk wallets are self-custodial. Walletalk does not hold your funds and cannot move them on its own.

  • Identity Wallet. The seed phrase and private keys are created on your device and never sent to Walletalk. If you lose the seed phrase, Walletalk cannot recover it.
  • Keyless Wallet. The key is split into three shares: one on your device, one in a recovery file encrypted with your backup password, and one held by Walletalk. The share held by Walletalk cannot sign transactions alone. If you lose both your devices and either the recovery file or the backup password, Walletalk cannot restore the wallet.
